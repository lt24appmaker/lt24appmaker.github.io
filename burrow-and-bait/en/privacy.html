Privacy Policy — Burrow & Bait

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Burrow & Bait (the "App") handles information. If this English version differs from any translation, the English version prevails.

1. Who we are

The App is published by LT24 App Maker ("we", "us"). Contact: lt24appmaker@gmail.com. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other data protection laws, LT24 App Maker is the controller of the limited data described below.

2. The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. The App never asks for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your game lives on your device. Your sanctuary, critters, inventory, field notebook and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any server and we cannot see your game data.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK. The App contains no advertising, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID).
  • Two service providers receive limited technical data so the App can work: RevenueCat (to confirm purchases) and Expo (app updates). Payments themselves are handled by Apple or Google.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

We use this only to answer you and delete support emails within 24 months after the conversation ends.

5. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): processing purchases and restoring the packs you bought; delivering app updates.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): detecting failed updates to keep the App working, and understanding aggregated sales. We minimised this data so it has little or no impact on you. You may object at any time (see section 7).
  • Consent: notifications are only shown if you allow them in the system permission prompt; you can withdraw this at any time in your device settings.

6. International transfers and retention

Our service providers are based in the United States and may process data there or in other countries. Where GDPR applies, transfers rely on the providers' safeguards, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where they participate and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in their policies.

Retention:

  • On-device data: until you delete it (Settings → Data → Delete all data) or uninstall the App.
  • RevenueCat: purchase records are kept while needed to validate and restore your purchases and to meet legal/accounting obligations, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Expo: update request records are kept according to Expo's policy; Expo states that raw request logs containing launch-error text are deleted after three days.
  • Support emails: deleted within 24 months after the conversation ends.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on your device: Settings → Data → Delete all data (removes game data, settings and scheduled notifications). Uninstalling the App also removes it. Purchases stay with your App Store / Google Play account and can be restored.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the App's settings or in your device's settings.
  • Purchases: view, manage or request refunds through your App Store or Google Play account.
  • Data held by our providers: because we do not know who you are, we usually cannot link provider data to you. If you want us to access or delete the purchase record held by RevenueCat, email us with the order ID from your Apple receipt or your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA."); we will locate and delete that record (packs may then need to be restored again from the store). Expo update records contain only a random client ID and cannot normally be linked to you.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. This includes rights under the GDPR (EEA/UK), California's CCPA/CPRA and other U.S. state privacy laws, Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection (No. 91/2025/QH15), Japan's APPI, Korea's PIPA and Brazil's LGPD. To exercise any right, email lt24appmaker@gmail.com. We will reply within the time required by applicable law (normally within 30 days). We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

California / U.S. states: In the preceding 12 months we have not sold or "shared" (for cross-context behavioural advertising) personal information, and we do not do so. The categories processed are limited to identifiers (random app/client IDs, IP address), commercial information (purchase history) and technical information (failed-update data), for the business purposes in section 4.

8. Security

Game data stays in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption). Network traffic to our providers is encrypted (HTTPS). The App does not store payment details. No method is 100% secure; please keep your device and store account protected.

9. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. It is not in the App Store Kids category or Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, give notice in the App or on the App's store page before the change takes effect.
