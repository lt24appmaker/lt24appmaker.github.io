Support — Burrow & Bait

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Contact: lt24appmaker@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few business days. Please include your device model, OS version and app version (Settings → Send feedback fills these in for you). Never send us your store password or payment details.

Frequently asked questions

1. I reinstalled the game / got a new phone. How do I get my purchases back?
Purchases belong to your App Store or Google Play account. Sign in to the same store account, connect to the internet, then tap Restore Purchases in the Shop or in Settings → Your packs. Purchases cannot move between Apple and Google devices.

2. How do I move my sanctuary to another phone?
There is no account or cloud sync, so your progress lives only on your device. On the old phone, go to Settings → Data → Export and save or send the backup file somewhere you can reach (for example Files, Google Drive or email to yourself). On the new phone, install the game, go to Settings → Data → Import, choose the file and confirm. Then restore purchases (question 1). Your device's own iCloud / Android backup may also carry your game over, but we can't guarantee it — exporting is the safe way.

3. How do I delete all my data?
Settings → Data → Delete all data, then confirm. This erases your game progress, settings and scheduled notifications on this device and cannot be undone — export a backup first if you might want it later. Your purchases are not lost; you can restore them anytime.

4. I'm not getting notifications.
Check that notifications are allowed for Burrow & Bait in your device settings and turned on in Settings → Notifications. By design, the game stays quiet between 22:00 and 08:00 (alerts move to the morning), sends at most 3 alert groups a day, and doesn't show alerts while you're playing. Notifications can arrive a few minutes late, especially with battery saver, Focus/Do Not Disturb, or aggressive battery optimisation on some Android phones. Your critters' results are always correct when you open the game, even if an alert didn't appear.

5. How do I change the language?
Settings → Language. The game is available in 15 languages: English, Tiếng Việt, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Русский, العربية, हिन्दी and Bahasa Indonesia. By default it follows your phone's language.

6. Can I get a refund?
Payments are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them:

  • App Store: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223 (reportaproblem.apple.com)
  • Google Play: https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637

If a purchase is refunded, its content is locked again but your progress is kept. All packs are one-time purchases — there is no subscription to cancel.

7. Does the game send my data anywhere?
Your game stays on your device. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. The game only contacts RevenueCat (to confirm purchases) and Expo (to download app updates), with limited technical data — there is no analytics or crash-reporting service. See our Privacy Policy for details: https://lt24appmaker.github.io/burrow-and-bait/en/privacy.html

8. Do I need to be online to play?
No. Everything works offline. Your traps and nests keep "running" while the game is closed and their results are worked out when you come back. You only need the internet to buy, restore purchases, and receive updates.

9. Something went wrong / I found a bug. How do I contact you?
Use Settings → Send feedback, or email lt24appmaker@gmail.com with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot. If your game shows an error screen when starting, please don't delete the app before contacting us — your save may still be recoverable on the device.
